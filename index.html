HOW TO PLAY

Enemies follow the road toward your command base. Some missions have two or three approach routes. Build towers on the glowing pads beside the routes. Air units ignore the road and fly straight to the base.

1 · PICK & PLACEClick a tower in the bottom bar. A ghost attaches to your cursor. Click a glowing pad to drop it.
2 · UPGRADE & TARGETNewly placed towers are selected automatically. Towers reach LV 2 on their own; LV 3 and LV 4 unlock in the Armory (tiers 1 and 3). Upgrade them, then cycle FIRST / STRONGEST / CLOSEST targeting.
3 · TARGETINGGunner and Cannon hit ground only. Missile, Laser, and Tesla hit air and ground.
4 · ROUTESLater missions have 2-3 enemy paths. Spread your towers to cover all approaches.
5 · ENDLESS MODEClear Mission 20 of a theater to unlock Endless Survival for that map. Waves scale infinitely.
EMP PULSE · EDisrupts every active hostile for 2 seconds and weakens energy shields. Recharges over 38 seconds.
SHIELDSWalkers and Titans carry energy shields. Laser towers pierce them; EMP drains a portion of shield energy.
ZOOM & PANMouse wheel or +/- keys to zoom. Right-drag or middle-drag to pan the map. WASD / Arrow keys also move. Press 0 to recenter.
HOVER PREVIEWHover a built tower to see its range. Hover a tower card in the bar to see full stats. Hover UPGRADE to preview next-level stats in green.
HOTKEYS1–5 towers · Space wave · E EMP · T target mode · F speed · U upgrade · X sell
